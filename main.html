Legal Metrology Guidelines

Important packaged commodity declarations checked by the system.

01 — Product Name

The package should clearly identify the name or description of the commodity.

02 — Net Quantity

The net quantity of the commodity should be declared using the appropriate unit of measurement.

03 — Maximum Retail Price

The maximum retail sale price should be declared on the package in the required manner.

04 — Manufacturer / Packer

Required manufacturer, packer or importer details should be available on the package.

05 — Consumer Information

Appropriate consumer-care or contact information should be declared where required.

MetroCheck is a software prototype designed to assist in checking packaged commodity declarations against Legal Metrology requirements.

The system uses image processing and OCR to extract information from product labels, followed by rule-based compliance analysis.
