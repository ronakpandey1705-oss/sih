Terms & Conditions

Standard operating conditions and legal terms governing use of the PackSure Legal Metrology Verification System.

Statutory Authority: Legal Metrology Act, 2009 & Packaged Commodities Rules, 2011
Effective Date: 01 September 2026
Version: 2.4.0-PROD

1. Statutory Framework and Purpose

PackSure is an automated screening and decision-support portal established to assist authorized Legal Metrology officers, state enforcement personnel, and internal compliance auditors in verifying packaged commodities against the statutory provisions of:

  • The Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (Act No. 1 of 2010).
  • The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (as amended).
  • Relevant mandatory Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) and FSSAI coordination schedules.

2. Scope of Assistive Automation

The system utilizes optical character recognition (OCR), rule-based heuristic validation, and catalog comparison engines to detect mandatory declarations under Rule 6 of the Packaged Commodities Rules, 2011. Users acknowledge that:

  • All automated outputs, risk scores, and violation flags are assistive and advisory in nature.
  • The automated screening score does not constitute a final compounding order, legal conviction, or conclusive judicial finding.
  • Physical packaging examination, test purchases, and statutory sampling procedures remain subject to the manual verification protocol prescribed by law.

3. Officer Discretion and Final Regulatory Authority

The inspecting officer retains sole statutory discretion and personal accountability for any regulatory action, including but not limited to:

  • Issuing statutory notices of non-compliance under Rule 32.
  • Authorizing physical seizure or compounding of offences under Sections 36, 48, and 49 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009.
  • Dismissing advisory flags where non-compliance is determined to be an artifact of photographic degradation, partial label obstruction, or permissible packaging exemption.

4. Evidentiary Record Integrity and Audit Logging

Inspection records, captured photographic evidence, OCR transcriptions, and officer determination notes are cryptographically indexed with session identifiers. Generated inspection notices and PDF reports constitute administrative audit documentation. Tampering with or altering saved inspection records is strictly prohibited under applicable electronic governance statutes.

5. Acceptable Use and Authorization

Access to this portal is authorized strictly for official regulatory audits, recognized manufacturing quality assurance routines, and authorized consumer protection evaluations. Unauthorized commercial exploitation, scraping of reference product databases, or injection of false label evidence is punishable under the Information Technology Act, 2000.

6. Disclaimer of Warranties and Limitation of Liability

The system is provided on an "as-is" basis. The administration makes no warranty that photographic label recognition will achieve 100 percent accuracy under suboptimal lighting, specular glare, or cylindrical surface distortion. Under no circumstances shall the software administration be liable for incidental or consequential damages resulting from administrative decisions made by users.

Data Protection Protocol

Privacy Policy

Policies governing evidence collection, photographic storage, OCR data handling, and regulatory confidentiality.

Data Controller: Legal Metrology Enforcement Directorate
Compliance Standard: Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023
Classification: Official Administrative Use Only

1. Categories of Information Processed

PackSure processes specific regulatory data categories strictly necessary for statutory packaging verification:

  • Photographic Evidence: High-resolution digital imagery of consumer product packaging, principal display panels, and mandatory label declaration panels.
  • Extracted Declaration Records: Transcribed OCR text containing product designations, manufacturer/packer/importer names and addresses, net quantities, maximum retail prices (MRP), unit sale prices, and consumer care coordinates.
  • Inspector & Premise Records: Officer ID tokens, establishment names, geographical inspection premises, and official determination remarks.

2. Image Processing and Local Server Storage

Photographs uploaded through the PackSure inspection station are transmitted securely to the server upload repository (backend/uploads/). Files are assigned unique cryptographic UUID identifiers to prevent naming collisions and unauthorized enumeration. Uploaded image files are retained solely for evidence logging, administrative review, and official report generation.

3. Absolute Prohibition on External Model Training

Uploaded packaging evidence, trade brand imagery, and proprietary manufacturer label data are strictly sequestered. PackSure enforces an absolute policy that no packaging photographs or extracted text records are shared with public AI models, scraped for commercial advertising, or utilized to train external public foundation models.

4. Client-Side Data and Local Storage

For operational continuity and offline audit capabilities, the web interface retains inspection session history in the browser's local storage (packsure_history). This data remains strictly within the inspector's local client device and can be cleared at any time via standard browser data management controls.

5. Security Protocols and Access Control

Technical safeguards implemented across the PackSure infrastructure include:

  • Strict MIME-type validation ensuring only legitimate photographic image payloads (JPEG, PNG, WebP) are processed.
  • Sanitization of all text extraction buffers to mitigate script injection and cross-site scripting (XSS).
  • Standard CORS policy restrictions limiting API invocations to authorized administrative domains.

6. Data Subject Rights and Contact

Inquiries regarding packaging data records, formal notice rectifications, or evidentiary data retention requests should be directed to the designated nodal officer at the Legal Metrology Division, Department of Consumer Affairs, New Delhi.

About PackSure

PackSure is an automated Legal Metrology compliance screening application engineered for Smart India Hackathon problem statement SIH26034.

System Purpose & Scope

Under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, every pre-packaged commodity distributed in the country must carry clear, legible, and unalterable mandatory declarations. PackSure accelerates field inspection workflows by automating barcode catalog matching, OCR declaration capture, statutory rule evaluation, and inspection notice compilation.
