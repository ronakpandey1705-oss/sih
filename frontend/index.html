PackSure

AI-assisted Legal Metrology screening for packaged commodities (SIH problem SIH26034). Officers look up a barcode, photograph the pack label, and review OCR, declaration extraction, rule flags, and catalog mismatches before recording a determination.

What this app does

  • Catalog lookup — match a barcode against the demo reference products, or continue if the pack is unknown.
  • Label photograph — upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP of the declarations on the pack.
  • Screening pipeline — OCR → field extraction → Legal Metrology rules → catalog discrepancy check.
  • Inspection record — generate a PDF notice and record the officer’s decision. Screening is assistive; the officer remains responsible for the final call.
